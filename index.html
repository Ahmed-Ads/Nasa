        How to Play

        1. Read the forecast. It tells you what is coming this sol — dust, a long shadow, or a solar storm.
        2. Spend your power cells. Tap + to send cells to a system. You can never spend more than the budget.
        3. Run the sol. The outpost powers up, the hazard hits, and every meter changes.
        4. Keep meters above 20%. Drop below and the crew loses a heart. Lose all 3 hearts, or let a meter hit 0, and the mission ends. Retry is free — and you get one Revive.

        Keyboard: 1 2 3 add a cell to that row, Shift+number removes one, Space runs the sol, B draws battery, R toggles water rationing. Walk with WASD / Arrows or click the Moon.

        Mission Science

        Every rule in this game starts from real space engineering. The numbers are simplified for ages 7–12 — anything invented for play is labelled game assumption.

        In-game ruleReal NASA scienceSimplification
        O₂ cells use water ISS oxygen comes from electrolysis of water (NASA ECLSS, Oxygen Generation Assembly) Each O₂ cell costs 3 water points (game assumption)
        Water recycling restores water ISS ECLSS recovers about 98% of water (2023 NASA ECLSS update) 1 cell = +9 water; no cell = water drains (game assumption)
        Leak repair ISS leak response: close hatches, locate the leak, Kapton tape, then epoxy. The 2018 Soyuz leak was a drilled hole, not a micrometeoroid Tape cuts the leak 60%; a permanent patch takes 3 repair cells
        Solar storm shelter Solar particle events: crews shelter in place; about 4 g/cm² of regolith is enough to get below 30-day exposure limits (NASA) Bunker = safe dose; shielding cells reduce the rest
        Cosmic rays Extra shielding barely reduces galactic cosmic rays — they are very high energy (NASA radiation research) Shielding only trims storm dose, never sets it to zero
        Long Shadow South-pole crater rims get near-constant sunlight, but terrain can shadow solar arrays (LRO / Diviner) “Long Shadow” event removes 2 solar cells for a sol
        Water ice events Shackleton crater holds water ice in permanently shadowed regions (LRO, LCROSS) Ice find = +12 water
