            How to Play

            1. 1. Plan power. Spend power cells. Leftover cells charge the battery.
            2. 2. Go outside. Walk with WASD or the joystick. Hold E or ACT to do a job. Suit air drains outside. It refills each day.
            3. 3. End the day. The base makes power. The crew uses supplies. Hazards happen.
            4. Keep everyone safe. Keep meters above 20%. Zero hearts ends the mission. Retry is free. You get one Revive.

            Stations: solar field (sweep dust, deploy a panel), battery bank, O₂/CO₂/water units (adjust cells on the spot), comms dish (detailed storm forecast), rover + ice patch (haul ice), bunker (shelter order), recycler (leak steps in 1.2).

            Mission Science

            Resources in Level 1

            Every rule starts from real space engineering. Values we could verify on NASA pages are marked real; everything tuned for play is marked game assumption.

            In-game ruleReal NASA scienceSimplification
            O₂ from waterISS oxygen comes from electrolysis of water (Oxygen Generation Assembly, ECLSS)Game: 1 O₂ cell = +10 O₂ and uses 2 water
            Water recyclingISS ECLSS recovers about 98% of water (2023 NASA update)Game: 1 water cell = +10 water
            Crew useNASA life-support baselines: crews need oxygen, water and food every dayGame: crew of 3 uses 10 O₂ and 6 water each day
            Leak responseISS procedure: close hatches, find the leak, Kapton tape, then epoxy. The 2018 Soyuz leak was a drilled hole — not a micrometeoroidGame: burst drains 30 water each day → isolate (40%) → tape (15%) → 2 repair cells → reopen
            Solar particle eventsCrews shelter in place; ~4 g/cm² of regolith gets below 30-day limits. CMEs take about 1–4 days to arrive (NASA DONKI)Game: the storm arrives on Day 3-5. Early shelter cuts dose by 90%.
            Cosmic raysExtra shielding barely reduces galactic cosmic raysGame: shielding slows dose but never zeroes it
            Long ShadowSouth-pole crater rims get near-constant sunlight; terrain can still shadow arraysGame: the event removes 2 solar cells for one day
            Lunar dustLunar dust is abrasive and degrades solar panels and seals (NASA lunar dust research)Game: dust event → sweep the panels outside restores output
            Water iceShackleton crater holds ice in permanently shadowed regions (LRO, LCROSS)Game: ice haul = +14 water

            Sources: NASA ECLSS / ISS, NASA Space Weather & DONKI, NASA Moon (LRO/Diviner/LCROSS), NASA radiation research. Full list in README.md. Educational model, not engineering-grade.
